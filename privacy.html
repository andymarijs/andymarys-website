Privacy policy · Family 365

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

Andy Marys BV, Kalvekeetdijk 67A, 8300 Knokke-Heist, Belgium. Company number BE 1021.521.054. Contact: andy@marijs.be.

The short version

  • There is no account. You do not give us your name, email address or phone number to use the app.
  • What you write down about your children (growth, health notes, firsts, pictures, art, awards, papers, pocket money, diaries) stays on your phone and the phones you link yourself. We do not receive it and cannot read it.
  • Messages, pictures, calls and the baby monitor go straight between your phones, encrypted. If your phones are on different networks, an optional relay passes along scrambled data that it cannot read.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell anything about you.

What the app keeps, and where

On your phone, in the app's own storage: the records you enter, pictures you add (they are not uploaded anywhere), the keys that protect your links to other phones (kept in the iPhone Keychain, only on that phone) and your choices. A child's private diary is locked with a code on the child's phone; no parent can read it. A backup file, if you make one, is locked with a passphrase only you know. You choose where it is saved. We cannot open it or recover a lost passphrase.

What travels between phones

Only between phones that were linked in person (a QR code and a check phrase), always encrypted with a key that only those phones hold: from a parent to a child's phone (calendar, plan, who picks the child up, pocket money); between the two parents of one family (the children's records and the plan); and, only if you switch it on, between families (group pictures you choose to send, and ride circles). In a ride circle other families see first names of children, the rides and who drives. Home addresses and the picture of the day are sealed so only the driver of that ride and the child's parents can read them.

The relay

When two phones are not on the same network, they can find each other through a small relay server. It passes along scrambled data and cannot read it. It does not store messages, pictures or the contents of your records. As with any internet connection, it necessarily sees the network address of a connecting phone while the connection is made.

Permissions

Camera and microphone: the baby monitor, calls, and pictures of art, papers and certificates. Speech recognition: voice notes and dictation, done on the phone. Photos: pictures you choose. Face ID: the child's diary. Local network: finding your own phones. Notifications: reminders you set. Nothing is switched on until you use the feature.

Children

The app is for parents and their children. Children's data is never shared outside the family and the friends and circles the parents choose. We show no advertising and build no profiles of children.

Not a medical device

Family 365 keeps notes and shows simple summaries. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor. The baby monitor does not replace supervision.

Your rights

Everything is on your devices, so you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app, and deleting the app removes it. If you write to us, we will tell you honestly what little we hold (for example, a purchase receipt through Apple, if purchases exist). You may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).

Status and changes

Family 365 is still in development. This policy describes how the app is designed to work and will be updated at launch. If it changes, the new version is shown here and in the app before it applies.
