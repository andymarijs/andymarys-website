How can we help?

Write to andy@marijs.be. We reply within a few working days.

Do I need an account?

No. Family 365 has no accounts. You link phones in person with a QR code.

Where is my data?

On your phone and the phones you link yourself. We do not receive it. Make a backup file from the menu, locked with a passphrase you keep.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app and everything on that phone goes with it. In the app you can also delete single entries.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Some features, such as the on-device assistant, need a newer iPhone.

Which languages?

Dutch, French and English.

I found a problem.

Please write to us with what you did and what you saw. A screenshot helps, but please leave real children's names out of it.
