Family 365

The only app your family will ever need.

From the first day to the day they leave home: follow how your children are doing, keep every memory, and never lose what matters. Built for busy working parents.

  • Growth and health in one place: growth curves, illness and medicine timer, vaccinations, emergency card, doctor's summary
  • Firsts, memories and letters to the future, art and awards kept for good, a yearly book
  • Calendar, lists, meals and agreements with your children, shared with the other parent
  • Ride circles: families you know take turns driving, everyone knows who picks up whom
  • A babysitter sheet, a custody schedule for parents who live apart, pocket money and shoe sizes
  • Ask your own records in plain language, answered on your phone
  • In Dutch, French and English

Private by design

There is no account. What you write about your children stays on your phone and the phones you link yourself, locked and encrypted. Calls and messages go straight between your phones. There is no advertising and no tracking.
